OBD Dash Widget brings live, customizable OBD-II gauge widgets directly to your Android homescreen.

Connect your Android device to your vehicle's OBD-II port via any ELM327-compatible Bluetooth, BLE, USB, or Wi-Fi adapter, and monitor real-time vehicle parameters (RPM, Speed, Coolant Temperature, Engine Load, Fuel Level, and more) right from your launcher.

Features:
* Homescreen Launcher Widgets: Analog Gauge (Speedometer-style with redline arc and red needle), Digital Number, and Live Trend Chart.
* Live Background Service: 3 Hz smooth widget updating in background.
* ECU Safety: Read-only protection on communication layer (no ECU write or fault clearing commands).
* Multi-Language Support: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Russian, Portuguese, Dutch, and more.
* Built-in Demo Mode: Test widgets without an adapter.

Note: Based on the open-source project AndrOBD by fr3ts0n. Refactored and modernized with AI assistance.
